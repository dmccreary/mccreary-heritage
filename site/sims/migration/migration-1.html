🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿 McCreary Family Migration 🇮🇪

From Scottish Lowlands to Ulster Plantation (c. 1610)

Map Legend

Scottish Origin Regions: Ayrshire, Galloway, Dumfriesshire
Ulster Destination Counties: Antrim, Down, Donegal, Tyrone
Migration Routes: Animated blue arrows (13 miles at narrowest crossing)

Historical Context: During the Ulster Plantation (beginning 1609), thousands of Lowland Scots, including McCreary families, migrated across the North Channel to settle on confiscated lands in Ulster. The short distance (only 13 miles at its narrowest) made this migration accessible for families seeking economic opportunity and religious freedom.
