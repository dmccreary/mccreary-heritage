About These Counties

During the Ulster Plantation (beginning 1609) and subsequent decades, McCreary families from Scotland settled primarily in these four counties of Ulster. The highlighted counties represent the main areas where Scottish Presbyterian families, including the McCrearys, established communities before many emigrated to North America in the early 18th century.

County Antrim

Closest to Scotland across the North Channel; major Presbyterian settlement area

County Down

Southeastern Ulster; significant Scottish Lowlander migration

County Donegal

Northwestern Ulster; mixed plantation settlement patterns

County Tyrone

Central Ulster; heart of the plantation scheme
