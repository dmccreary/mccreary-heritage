!!! prompt
    Please help me design a 14-panel graphic novel to explain why the McCreary ancestors were invited into Northern Ireland after Cromwell's Conquest but finally left for America.
    For each panel, give a detailed description of contents of the panel and a short text narrative that should be placed after the panel. 

    Describe the challenges of living in Ireland around 1600 before the Irish Rebellion of 1641.

    Describe how the Ulster Plantation plan was devised and McCreary's from Scotland moved to Northern Ireland.

    Describe the the Irish Rebellion of 1641.

    Tell why Oliver Cromwell's started brutal military campaign to reconquer Ireland from 1649-1653 

    Describe how Cromwell's forces besieged and captured major towns, with infamous massacres at Drogheda and Wexford. 

    Describe the  conquest resulted in massive confiscation of Catholic-owned land (reducing Catholic landholding from 60% to 20% of Ireland), execution or exile of Irish leaders, and establishment of Protestant control. 

    Describe the reaction of the local Catholic people to the Ulster Plantation plan.

    Describe the challenges the McCreary's had along with other Presbyterians in Ireland finally decide to migrate to America.

    Use the following format for each panel.
