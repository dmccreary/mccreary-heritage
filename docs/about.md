# About This Book

This book is an online resources for teaching the family history of people with the surname `McCreary` and related spellings.  It was inspired by my travels to Scotland and my awareness of the very few online interactive intelligent textbooks that taught family history.

It is intended to be used in schools without fees and is licensed under [Creating Commons](./license.md).

Much of the content and applications were generated by large-language models.  We describe this process in detail in our [Prompts](./prompts/index.md).  The goal is to allow these prompts to be used by other educators teaching history.

Although the McCreary clan is not directly related to the MacQuarrie Clan, I have also created a textbook for them after seeing that their content was difficult to manage and did not include interactive timelines of events.
